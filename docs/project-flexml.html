FlexML

Turn your iPhone into a real-time AI vision system.

FlexML reframes the smartphone as an accessible robotics component: a camera, inference engine, interface, and communications layer bundled into one familiar device.

Swift Student Challenge Winner Developer

Hook

Use what people already have in their pocket.

The iPhone is powerful, reliable, and camera-rich. FlexML uses that advantage instead of forcing robotics teams to carry extra edge hardware.

Problem

Robotics teams often depend on Raspberry Pi or NVIDIA Jetson setups for on-device vision. That adds cost, thermal management issues, power complexity, and fragile setup time that should be spent on the robot itself.

Approach

  • Designed a Swift-based iOS workflow for loading Core ML models directly on device.
  • Built an AVFoundation-powered camera pipeline optimized for real-time inference.
  • Enabled dynamic model deployment and streaming of data toward external robotics hardware.
  • Shaped the product around clarity, with simple model selection and setup steps.

Your role

Developer. I handled the concept, product framing, iOS implementation, model deployment workflow, and the technical story around why the phone should become the robotics node.

Impact

  • Reached around 30 FPS through an optimized on-device inference path.
  • Reduced the need for extra compute hardware in small robotics workflows.
  • Connected product intuition with engineering depth, which is why it became the portfolio hero.

Learning

FlexML taught me that high-end engineering only becomes compelling when the entry point is simple. The most important work was not just model execution; it was translating capability into a product people could approach confidently.
