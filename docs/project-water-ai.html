Projects / Water AI

AI-Based Water Quality Assessment

Use RGB imaging to screen water quality without direct contact.

Water AI explores how accessible imaging and classification can support faster, lower-friction checks on water samples gathered from real environments.

Hook

Bring AI closer to field conditions.

The core challenge is not only model accuracy. It is building a data collection and imaging setup that remains useful outside polished lab conditions.

Problem

Water testing workflows can be slow, specialized, or hard to scale in field settings. I wanted to explore whether image-based classification could support a more accessible first-pass assessment.

Approach

  • Collected real water samples from Vijayawada and Amaravathi.
  • Paired dataset creation with lab validation for stronger grounding.
  • Built a physical imaging setup to control sample capture conditions.
  • Implemented preprocessing, augmentation, and inference steps for classification.

Your role

Developer. I contributed across data collection, imaging workflow design, preprocessing, and the inference pipeline itself.

Impact

  • Built the project around real samples instead of synthetic assumptions.
  • Created a working imaging rig that linked hardware setup with AI inference.
  • Expanded my ability to design AI systems where data quality depends on physical construction.

Learning

Physical AI projects are only as strong as the capture process. This case study made it clear that model performance, hardware arrangement, and environmental noise all need to be designed together.
